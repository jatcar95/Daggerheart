Part swamp, part expansive city, the North Marshes are a vast area in northeast of [[The Meld]] teeming with life and community. 

Initially formed hundreds of years ago when multiple druid enclaves decided to band together, the network of communities has steadily expanded ever since. While it still is predominantly made up of druids enclaves, it also encompasses communities of faeries, galapas, and ribbets. 

Due to a largely fabricated food crisis, the North Marshes were the predominant target of [[Ravaldia|Ravaldia's]] initial invasions.
